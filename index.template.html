Who We Are

Protecting lives. Empowering safety and the environment.

RIM-HSE — Réseau d'Ingénierie Mauritanien pour la Santé, la Sécurité et l'Environnement — is a Mauritanian-owned HSE consultancy and training company, established in 2017 and headquartered in Nouakchott. We bridge the gap between international safety standards and local operational requirements, helping clients mitigate risk, ensure compliance, and prioritise their most valuable asset: their people.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality HSE training, consulting, and safety solutions that help businesses meet international standards, reduce risks, and build a sustainable culture of safety.

Our Vision

To be the trusted leader in health, safety, and environmental excellence across Mauritania and West Africa — empowering organizations to protect lives, property, and the planet.

Integrity

We uphold the highest ethical standards in everything we do, ensuring trust and transparency with our clients and partners.

Responsibility

We believe in protecting people and the environment by promoting safe work practices and regulatory compliance.

Partnership

We work hand-in-hand with clients, regulators, and stakeholders to achieve shared goals in HSE performance.

Innovation

We embrace technology and best practices to deliver effective, forward-thinking HSE solutions.
